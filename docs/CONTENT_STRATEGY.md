# Content strategy

Voice: execution-serious, India-first, commercially honest. Parent company facts come from sonilbuildcon.com (legal entity, Indore HQ, sectors, phone/email). Nothing else is invented for proof.

## Positioning

**EHS + ESG + Regulatory Compliance — One Platform.** Field safety, risk, statutory tracking, sustainability reporting, and management analytics share one tenant. Self-hosting is an enterprise deployment option.

## What we write

- How the product actually works (field → workspace → entitlements)
- Indian reporting language (BRSR, EPR, Factories Act) as **orientation**, with “confirm with counsel / CS / auditor”
- Industry pages that keep distinct operational problems (no mail-merged plant names)

## What we do not write

- Fake customers, logos, quotes, case studies, awards
- Fake certifications or SLA numbers
- Fake prices or “free forever” trials
- “We file with SEBI / CPCB / MCA”
- A second compliance rules engine in marketing copy

## Partial product (say so)

Contractor management is a register, not a contractor OS. Training is in progress with the product team. Demo/contact forms are placeholders. Compare pages stay drafts.
