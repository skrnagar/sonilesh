LMRA Reporting
Important Note:
• LMRA stands for Last Minute Risk Assessment.
• This is applicable for all the activities at site.
There are Two steps in reporting LMRA and Closure:
Step1 LMRA Entry The User (Site Engineer/Supervisor/Foreman) 
must fill the details of the site, Risks identified 
while performing the activity and their 
controls taken, in the LMRA Entry and must 
submit for the ESHO approval.
Step 2 LMRA Approval The ESHO then Approve/Reject the LMRA after 
detailed verification.
