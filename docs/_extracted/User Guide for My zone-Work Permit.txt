Points To Remember before filling the Work Permit
1.User of Permit : Site Engineer/Supervisor/Foreman (KEC)
2.Issuer of Permit: Section In-Charge (Area In-Charge/HOD/Construction Manager)
or in case of absence of Section In-Charge, Project Manager (PM)
will be issuer
3. Reviewer/Assessor of Permit: Safety Officer (KEC)
4. Closure of Permit: User- Site Engineer/Supervisor/Foreman (KEC)
5. Important Notes: • Permit duration should not exceed 12 hrs., in case work
extension required- User has access to request for extension
for work permit max. 12 hours before closing of work permit
with proper approval.
• Once the permit got rejected/cancelled, again same has to re-
issue after rectification of remarks.
• It is important to close work permit before validity time as it
will expire immediately after validity time.
• Any uncheck points in permit checklist will be considered as
“Not done”.

--- PAGE 7 ---
Issuer’s flow
• The issuer shall Approve the permit, if there
is no deviation or Reject if there is any.
• After reviewing, the Permit Issuer should
forward the Permit to ESHO in “Review by
EHSO” field.
• The issuer could write the Remarks regarding
the permit.
• In the Work Permit List, the status of the permit will
be shown as APPROVED after approval and Rejected
if rejected by Issuer.
• Mail communication with pdf will be sent accordingly
as To “Safety Officer”, “User” , and CC “Issuer” and (If
approved)
• Mail communication with pdf will be sent accordingly
as To “User” and CC “Issuer” and “Safety Officer”. (If
Rejected)


--- PAGE 8 ---
Safety Officer’s flow
• The ESHO will verify the Permit
and shall click OKAY, if there is
no deviation and Cancel if there
is any.
• The ESHO shall write the remarks
regarding the PERMIT and shall
click on Verify Permit
• In the Work Permit List, the
status of the permit will be
shown as Verified by EHS &
Cancelled by EHS accordingly.


--- PAGE 9 ---
Extension Flow (If- Needed)
User Flow
• The User can extend the Permit
if the activity will go beyond the
validity time by clicking on
Extend Permit.
ESHO Flow
• The ESHO can verify the Permit
extension by clicking on Verify
Permit Extension and can cancel
the extension if any deviation
found.
Issuer Flow
• The Issuer can approve the
extension of the Permit if the
activity will go beyond the
stipulated time by clicking on
“Approve Extension”.


--- PAGE 10 ---
User’s Flow – Closure of permit
• Closure of the Permit shall be
done by user by clicking on Close
Permit
• The User shall select the
appropriate action from the box,
and write the Closure Remarks
and then click on Close Permit
after completion of the work.
• The status of the permit will be
displayed as CLOSED
