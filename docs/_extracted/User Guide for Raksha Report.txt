RAKSHA is a digital platform for EHS management and compliance. Starting  
from reporting of Unsafe act/condition & Incidents to Safety MIS, HSV, RSV  
& trainings. RAKSHA facilitates faster communication and better  
collaboration within the organization in order achieve “Zero Injury” and “Zero  
Accident” goal.
Now you can access RAKSHA web portal and download the app  
on Android and IOS https://digital.kecrpg.com

• Download and Open the app
• Click on ‘Sign in’button
• Choose your user type
