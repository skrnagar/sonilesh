TSV Closed Cases
21
Then Safety officer & Project Manager will review the Observation Closure 
points and Pictures which is attached by User. Then-
Case 1:-
If Observation Closure points & attached picture is “RELEVANT” against the 
observation point then Project Manager will close the TSV.(If Both are satisfied 
with closure points).
“That’s means TSV is CLOSED” .
Case 2:-
If Observation Closure points & attached picture is “NOT RELEVANT “against 
the observation (If Both are not satisfied with closure points), then Safety 
Officer will again Allocate the Action item( Observation point) to the User. 
“That’s means Observation is NOT CLOSED (TSV is still open)”.
